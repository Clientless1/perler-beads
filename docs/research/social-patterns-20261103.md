# Pattern research — 2026-11-03

Reviewed the public [Douyin search for 拼豆图纸](https://www.douyin.com/search/%E6%8B%BC%E8%B1%86%E5%9B%BE%E7%BA%B8) in a browser. Visible search topics include 可爱、小型、小图、简单、情侣 and character fan art. Visible creators also explicitly limit use: 金欠JinQian requests authorization for commercial use and commissioned beading; AnanDC says 严禁二传 / 无授权商用; 没烦恼DIY拼豆 says 禁止商用. These pages were used to understand subject preferences; no creator image, video or pattern was downloaded or republished.

Selected [Twemoji v14.0.2](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/v14.0.2) instead: 256 distinct animal, plant, fruit, food, dessert, expression and object subjects. Graphics are explicitly CC BY 4.0 in the project's README and LICENSE-GRAPHICS. Downloaded the pinned public PNGs and license from raw.githubusercontent.com, retained originals and SHA-256 checksums, and converted the graphics into 29-cell MARD patterns. Each is cropped, resized to at most 25 visible cells, thresholded for transparent edges and limited to 12 MARD colors. These are adaptations of emoji art, not imported third-party bead charts and not claims of Douyin popularity.

Attribution is retained in the source sheet, template detail, result view, local saved history and both kinds of exported PNG. Export includes the author, CC BY 4.0, the source URL, license URL and modification notice.

Also inspected public GitHub repository search for bead tools. Kenney's robots.txt disallows automated asset paths, and the current Noto Emoji license/layout is inconsistent across resources; neither is used for new assets. No further automated retrieval of those collections was performed after inspection.
